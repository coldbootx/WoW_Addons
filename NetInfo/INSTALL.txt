NetInfo v1.1 Classic Era WoW Installation

1. Download the NetInfo Classic folder.
2. Copy the entire folder to your WoW Classic AddOns directory:
   World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\
3. Ensure the following files are present:
   - NetInfo.lua
   - Locales\*.lua
   - NetInfo.toc
4. Launch WoW Classic and enable NetInfo from the AddOns menu.
5. Login and the frame will appear automatically.
6. Use /netinfo help for slash commands.
